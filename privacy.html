SMS Privacy Policy

Effective August 1, 2026

This policy explains how DallasClounch.com and DARA's coordination line handle information for vendor, home-service, scheduling, and support communication.

Information collected

  • Name, phone number, email address, vendor or appointment details, service notes, and the contents of calls, texts, emails, or form messages you send.
  • Technical records needed to operate communication services, keep accurate logs, and respond to support requests.

How information is used

  • To schedule appointments, coordinate vendor work, respond to support requests, keep accurate records, and follow up about active service needs.
  • SMS consent is used only to communicate with you about the conversation or service you initiated or agreed to receive.
  • Mobile opt-in data, SMS consent, and phone numbers will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • Information may be shared only when needed to complete coordination you requested, comply with law, protect safety, or operate the communication services used by DARA.

Choices and contact

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your information by emailing dallas@theaisurfer.com. For SMS help, reply HELP. To stop SMS messages, reply STOP.

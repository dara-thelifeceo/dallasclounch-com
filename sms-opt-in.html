SMS opt-in

How text consent works

DARA only texts contacts who initiate a conversation, provide their phone number for coordination, or confirm they want DARA to coordinate by SMS.

Opt-in flow

  1. A vendor or contact starts the conversation, provides their number, or confirms that DARA may coordinate by text.
  2. DARA states the purpose: vendor coordination, scheduling, appointment logistics, and support.
  3. DARA provides opt-out language: reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.
  4. The contact can continue the coordination thread, or opt out at any time.

Example

Contact: “You can text me here about Dallas’s appointment.”

DARA: “Thanks. DARA uses SMS only for vendor coordination, scheduling, appointment logistics, and support. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Msg/data rates may apply.”

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing goods or services. Mobile opt-in data, SMS consent, and phone numbers are not sold or shared for marketing.
